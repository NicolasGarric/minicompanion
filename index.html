MiniCompanion — from the first brushstroke to the first battle

A free, independent companion for miniature and tabletop wargaming hobbyists. Plan the colours of your miniature before opening a pot, find a tournament or a game near you, and share your work.

Paint

Upload a photo of your miniature: the app extracts its palette, matches the closest paints, and lets you try base coats, washes, drybrushing and edge highlights directly on your own model. Share the scheme as a card or a link. A beginner mode gives five steps, a shopping list and a store nearby.

Play

Find tournaments, quick casual games, leagues and painting contests. Organisers get Swiss pairings, live standings, QR codes for score entry at each table, and multi-week events with a date and venue per round.
